# 🏛️ Seventeen Centuries

Three books, seventeen centuries apart, as one knowledge graph: Marcus Aurelius's *Meditations* (around 170 AD), Machiavelli's *The Prince* (1513) and Nietzsche's *Beyond Good and Evil* (1886). Every aphorism, passage and chapter is its own document, and more than 300 concept documents (virtue, power, fortune, the will to power, Socrates, Caesar) link the places where the three authors meet.

## Start here

1. Open a concept from [Categories](categories.md), such as virtue or power, and read how each author uses it. Its *Mentioned In* section links every passage that does.
2. Read [Virtue across seventeen centuries](articles/virtue-across-centuries.md), an essay written on top of the graph, to see what the graph is for.
3. Ask your agent a question that crosses the books, such as how Marcus and Machiavelli differ on fortune. It answers from the passages and links the ones it quotes.
