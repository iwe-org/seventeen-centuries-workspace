# 🏛️ Seventeen Centuries

Three books, seventeen centuries apart, as one knowledge graph: Marcus Aurelius's *Meditations* (around 170 AD), Machiavelli's *The Prince* (1513) and Nietzsche's *Beyond Good and Evil* (1886). Every paragraph, chapter and aphorism is its own document, 843 passages in all, and 331 concept documents link the passages that treat an idea, a person or a school. 42 of them, among them virtue, fortune, death, power and justice, link passages from all three books and compare what each author does with the word; 13 link two books; the rest belong to one book, most of them to Nietzsche.

## Start here

1. Open [Fortune](concepts/fortune.md) or another concept from [Categories](categories.md). Its summary compares the authors, and *Mentioned In* lists the passages by book.
2. Read [Virtue across seventeen centuries](articles/virtue-across-centuries.md), an essay written on top of the graph, to see what the graph is for.
3. Ask your agent a question that crosses the books, such as how Marcus Aurelius and Machiavelli differ on fortune, or what each of the three says about death. It reads the concept, searches each book, answers from the passages it quotes, and says when an author has nothing on it.
4. Ask your agent to keep a note on a passage. Notes are collected in [Notes](notes.md); the passages themselves are never edited.

## Sources

- *Meditations*, Marcus Aurelius, translated by George Long (1862), from the [Standard Ebooks edition](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/marcus-aurelius/meditations/george-long). That edition prints no section numbers, so the passages are numbered by paragraph, and a number can differ from other editions.
- *The Prince*, Niccolò Machiavelli, translated by W. K. Marriott (1908), from the [Standard Ebooks edition](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/niccolo-machiavelli/the-prince/w-k-marriott).
- *Beyond Good and Evil*, Friedrich Nietzsche, translated by Helen Zimmern (1906), from the [Standard Ebooks edition](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/friedrich-nietzsche/beyond-good-and-evil/helen-zimmern).
