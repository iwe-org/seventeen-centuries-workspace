# BGE 35

O [Voltaire](../concepts/voltaire.md)! O humanity! O idiocy! There is something ticklish in "the [truth](../concepts/truth.md)," and in the *search* for the truth; and if man goes about it too humanely—"*il ne cherche le vrai que pour faire le bien*"—I wager he finds nothing!
