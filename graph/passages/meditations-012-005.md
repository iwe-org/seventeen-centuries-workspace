# Meditations XII.5

and if thou shalt strive to live only what is really thy [life](../concepts/life.md), that is, the present—then thou wilt be able to pass that portion of life which remains for thee up to the time of thy death, free from perturbations, nobly, and [obedient](../concepts/obedience.md) to thy own daemon [to the god that is within thee].
