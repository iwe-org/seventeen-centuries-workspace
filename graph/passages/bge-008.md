# BGE 8

There is a point in every [Philosophy](../concepts/philosophy.md) at which the "[Conviction](../concepts/conviction.md)" of the [Philosopher](../concepts/philosopher.md) appears on the scene; or, to put it in the words of an ancient mystery:

> Adventavit asinus,Pulcher et fortissimus.
