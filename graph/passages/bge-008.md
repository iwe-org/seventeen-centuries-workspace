# BGE 8

There is a point in every [philosophy](../concepts/philosophy.md) at which the "[conviction](../concepts/conviction.md)" of the [philosopher](../concepts/philosophy.md) appears on the scene; or, to put it in the words of an ancient mystery:

> Adventavit asinus,\
> Pulcher et fortissimus.
