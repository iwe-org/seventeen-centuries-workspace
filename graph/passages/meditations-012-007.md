# Meditations XII.7

How can it be that the [gods](../concepts/god.md) after having arranged all things well and benevolently for mankind, have overlooked this alone, that some men and very good men, and men who, as we may say, have had most communion with the divinity, and through pious acts and [religious](../concepts/religion.md) observances have been most intimate with the divinity, when they have once died should never exist again, but should be completely extinguished?
