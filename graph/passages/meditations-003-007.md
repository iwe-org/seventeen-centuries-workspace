# Meditations III.7

Never value anything as profitable to thyself which shall compel thee to break thy promise, to lose thy self-respect, to [hate](../concepts/hatred.md) any man, to suspect, to curse, to act the [hypocrite](../concepts/honesty.md), to desire anything which needs walls and curtains: for he who has preferred to everything intelligence and daemon and the worship of its excellence, acts no tragic part, does not groan, will not need either [solitude](../concepts/solitude.md) or much company; and, what is chief of all, he will live without either pursuing or flying from [death](../concepts/death.md); but whether for a longer or a shorter time he shall have the soul enclosed in the body, he cares not at all: for even if he must depart immediately, he will go as readily as if he were going to do anything else which can be done with decency and order; taking care of this only all through life, that his thoughts turn not away from anything which belongs to an intelligent animal and a member of a civil community.
