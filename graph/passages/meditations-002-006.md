# Meditations II.6

Do wrong to thyself, do wrong to thyself, my [soul](../concepts/soul.md); but thou wilt no longer have the opportunity of honouring thyself. Every man's life is sufficient. But thine is nearly finished, though thy soul reverences not itself but places thy [felicity](../concepts/happiness.md) in the souls of others.
