# Meditations IX.27

When another blames thee or [hates](../concepts/hatred.md) thee, or when men say about thee anything injurious, approach their poor souls, penetrate within, and see what kind of men they are. Thou wilt discover that there is no reason to take any trouble that these men may have this or that [opinion](../concepts/opinion.md) about thee. However thou must be well disposed towards them, for by nature they are [friends](../concepts/friendship.md). And the gods too aid them in all ways, by dreams, by signs, towards the attainment of those things on which they set a value.
