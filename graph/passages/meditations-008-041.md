# Meditations VIII.41

In the constitution of the rational animal I see no [virtue](../concepts/virtue.md) which is opposed to [justice](../concepts/justice.md); but I see a virtue which is opposed to love of [pleasure](../concepts/pleasure-and-pain.md), and that is [temperance](../concepts/self-mastery.md).
