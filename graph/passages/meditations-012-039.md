# Meditations XII.39

Man, thou hast been a citizen in this great state [the world]; what difference does it make to thee whether for five years [or three]? for that which is conformable to the [laws](../concepts/law.md) is just for all. Where is the hardship then, if no tyrant nor yet an [unjust](../concepts/justice.md) judge sends thee away from the state, but nature who brought thee into it? the same as if a prætor who has employed an actor dismisses him from the stage.—"But I have not finished the five acts, but only three of them."—Thou sayest well, but in [life](../concepts/life.md) the three acts are the whole drama; for what shall be a complete drama is determined by him who was once the cause of its composition, and now of its [dissolution](../concepts/death.md): but thou art the cause of neither. Depart then satisfied, for he also who releases thee is satisfied.
