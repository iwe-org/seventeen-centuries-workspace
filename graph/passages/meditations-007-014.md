# Meditations VII.14

Let there fall externally what will on the parts which can feel the effects of this fall. For those parts which have felt will complain, if they choose. But I, unless I think that what has happened is an [evil](../concepts/good-and-evil.md), am not injured. And it is in my power not to think so.
