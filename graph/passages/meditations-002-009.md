# Meditations II.9

This thou must always bear in mind, what is the [nature](../concepts/nature.md) of the whole, and what is my nature, and how this is related to that, and what kind of a part it is of what kind of a whole; and that there is no one who hinders thee from always doing and saying the things which are according to the nature of which thou art a part.
