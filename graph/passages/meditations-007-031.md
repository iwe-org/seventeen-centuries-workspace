# Meditations VII.31

Adorn thyself with simplicity and modesty and with indifference towards the things which lie between [virtue](../concepts/virtue.md) and vice. [Love](../concepts/love.md) mankind. Follow God. The poet says that [Law](../concepts/law.md) rules all. And it is enough to remember that Law rules all.
