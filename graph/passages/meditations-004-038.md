# Meditations IV.38

Observe constantly that all things take place by [change](../concepts/change.md), and accustom thyself to consider that the [nature](../concepts/nature.md) of the Universe loves nothing so much as to change the things which are and to make new things like them. For everything that exists is in a manner the seed of that which will be. But thou art thinking only of seeds which are cast into the earth or into a womb: but this is a very vulgar notion.
