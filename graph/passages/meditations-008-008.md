# Meditations VIII.8

Thou hast not leisure [or ability] to read. But thou hast leisure [or ability] to check [arrogance](../concepts/vanity.md); thou hast leisure to be superior to pleasure and pain; thou hast leisure to be superior to love of fame, and not to be [vexed](../concepts/anger.md) at stupid and [ungrateful](../concepts/gratitude.md) people, nay even to care for them.
