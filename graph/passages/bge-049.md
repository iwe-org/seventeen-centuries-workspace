# BGE 49

That which is so astonishing in the religious life of the [Ancient Greeks](../concepts/ancient-greeks.md) is the irrestrainable stream of *[Gratitude](../concepts/gratitude.md)* which it pours forth—it is a very superior kind of man who takes *such* an attitude towards nature and life.—Later on, when the populace got the upper hand in Greece, *[Fear](../concepts/fear.md)* became rampant also in religion; and [Christianity](../concepts/christianity.md) was preparing itself.
