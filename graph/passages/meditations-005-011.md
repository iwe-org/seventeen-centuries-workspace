# Meditations V.11

About what am I now employing my own [soul](../concepts/soul.md)? On every occasion I must ask myself this question, and inquire, what have I now in this part of me which they call the [ruling principle](../concepts/reason.md)? And whose soul have I now? That of a child, or of a young man, or of a feeble woman, or of a tyrant, or of a domestic animal, or of a wild beast?
