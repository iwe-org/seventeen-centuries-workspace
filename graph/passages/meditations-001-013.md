# Meditations I.13

From Catulus, not to be indifferent when a [friend](../concepts/friendship.md) finds fault, even if he should find fault without reason, but to try to restore him to his usual disposition; and to be ready to speak well of teachers, as it is reported of Domitius and Athenodotus; and to [love](../concepts/love.md) my children truly.
