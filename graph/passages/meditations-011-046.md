# Meditations XI.46

Neither in writing nor in reading wilt thou be able to lay down rules for others before thou shalt have first learned to [obey](../concepts/obedience.md) rules thyself. Much more is this so in life.

> A slave thou art: free speech is not for thee.\
> —And my heart laughed within.
