# Meditations XII.32

The safety of life is this, to examine everything all through, what it is itself, what is its material, what the formal part; with all thy soul to do justice and to say the [truth](../concepts/truth.md). What remains except to enjoy life by joining one good thing to another so as not to leave even the smallest intervals between?
