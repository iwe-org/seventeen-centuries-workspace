# BGE 53

Why [Atheism](../concepts/atheism.md) nowadays? "The father" in [God](../concepts/god.md) is thoroughly refuted; equally so "the judge," "the rewarder." Also his "[free will](../concepts/free-will.md)": he does not hear—and even if he did, he would not know how to help. The worst is that he seems incapable of communicating himself clearly; is he uncertain?—This is what I have made out (by questioning and listening at a variety of conversations) to be the cause of the decline of European theism; it appears to me that though the [religious instinct](../concepts/religious-instinct.md) is in vigorous growth—it rejects the theistic satisfaction with profound distrust.
