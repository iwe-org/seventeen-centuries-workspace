# Meditations VII.26

When a man has done thee any wrong, immediately consider with what opinion about good or [evil](../concepts/good-and-evil.md) he has done wrong. For when thou hast seen this, thou wilt [pity](../concepts/sympathy.md) him, and wilt neither wonder nor be [angry](../concepts/anger.md). For either thou thyself thinkest the same thing to be good that he does or another thing of the same kind. It is thy [duty](../concepts/duty.md) then to pardon him. But if thou dost not think such things to be good or evil, thou wilt more readily be well disposed to him who is in error.
