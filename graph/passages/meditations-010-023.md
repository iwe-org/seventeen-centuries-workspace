# Meditations X.23

"The earth [loves](../concepts/love.md) the shower"; and "the solemn æther loves": and the universe loves to make whatever is about to be. I say then to the universe, that I love as thou lovest. And is not this too said, that "this or that loves [is wont] to be produced"?
