# BGE 18

It is certainly not the least charm of a [Theory](../concepts/theory.md) that it is refutable; it is precisely thereby that it attracts the more subtle minds. It seems that the hundred-times-refuted theory of the "[Free Will](../concepts/free-will.md)" owes its persistence to this charm alone; someone is always appearing who feels himself strong enough to refute it.
