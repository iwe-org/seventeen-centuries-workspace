# Order of Rank

## Summary

Nietzsche holds that there is an order of rank between man and man, and therefore between morality and morality. Philosophers once believed in gradations of rank and not in equality and equal rights — among the Indians, Greeks, Persians and Mussulmans — and from that belief came the distinction of the exoteric and the esoteric, of seeing things from below and from above. The "requirement of one morality for all is really a detriment to higher men", because what is fair to one may not be fair to another. What settles rank is not actions or works but belief — the fundamental certainty a noble soul has about itself.
