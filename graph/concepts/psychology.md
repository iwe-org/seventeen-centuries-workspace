# Psychology

## Summary

Nietzsche holds that psychology has so far run aground on moral prejudices and fears and has not dared to descend into the depths. It must give up the old soul-atomism for conceptions such as "soul as social structure of the instincts and passions" and be understood as the morphology and development-doctrine of the Will to Power; then it is again the queen of the sciences and the path to the fundamental problems. Its practitioner, the born psychologist, is a lover of the "big hunt" whose hunting-domain is the human soul and its history — and who finds that he must do everything himself, because scholars lose their keen eye and nose exactly where the danger begins.
