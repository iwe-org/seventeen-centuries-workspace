# Prayer

## Summary

Prayer is characterized as a "soft placidity" and a state of perpetual readiness for the "coming of God." It is presented as a practice that requires outward idleness or semi-idleness, belonging to the contemplative religious life that modern laboriousness has rendered increasingly difficult to maintain.
