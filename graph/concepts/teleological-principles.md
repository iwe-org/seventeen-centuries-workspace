# Teleological Principles

## Summary

Nietzsche warns against superfluous teleological principles in psychology, citing self-preservation as a prime example of such unnecessary explanatory concepts. Rather than being a cardinal instinct, self-preservation is merely an indirect result of the more fundamental drive: the will to power, which seeks above all to discharge its strength.
