# Synthetic Judgments A Priori

## Summary

Nietzsche counts synthetic judgments a priori among the falsest opinions and the most indispensable: without logical fictions and a constant counterfeiting of the world by numbers man could not live, and to recognise untruth as a condition of life is already to stand beyond good and evil. He mocks Kant's pride in having discovered a faculty for them — to answer "how are they possible?" with "by means of a faculty" is the opium that induces sleep by its dormitive virtue — and replaces the question with another: why is belief in such judgments necessary for creatures like ourselves?
