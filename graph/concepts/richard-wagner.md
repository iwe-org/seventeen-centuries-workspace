# Richard Wagner

## Summary

Nietzsche presents Wagner both as an expression of the German soul and as a European event. With Hegel, who put its riddles into a system, Wagner set the contradictory German soul to music; the overture to the Mastersingers is magnificent, heavy, latter-day art, at once ancient and modern. He counts Wagner among the great synthesizers who prepared the European of the future, close kin to late French romanticism, and turns on the late Wagner whose Parsifal preaches the way to Rome.
