# Creation of Values

## Summary

To create values is, for Nietzsche, the mark of both the real philosopher and the noble type. Philosophical workers fix and formalize what has already been valued; the real philosophers are commanders and lawgivers who say "thus shall it be" — their knowing is creating, their creating is a law-giving, their will to truth is Will to Power. In master-morality the noble man regards himself as the determiner of values: he needs no approval, judges that what is injurious to him is injurious in itself, and knows that it is he who confers honour on things — "he is a creator of values."
