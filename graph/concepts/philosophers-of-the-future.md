# Philosophers of the Future

## Summary

The philosophers of the future — the "new philosophers" — are the type Nietzsche sees coming. They will be free, very free spirits, the opposite of the levelling "freethinkers" who want the green-meadow happiness of the herd; experimenters and critics in body and soul who will not be called critics. Strong and original enough to give the impulse to opposite valuations and to transvalue eternal ones, they are to teach man that his future is his will and to end the "frightful rule of folly and chance" that has been called history — which is why their possible absence or miscarriage is Nietzsche's real anxiety.
