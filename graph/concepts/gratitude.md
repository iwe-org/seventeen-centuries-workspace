# Gratitude

## Summary

Gratitude characterizes the religious life of the ancient Greeks, who poured forth an irrestrainable stream of thankfulness toward nature and life. Nietzsche considers this attitude the mark of a superior kind of man, contrasting it with the fear that later dominated religion when the populace gained influence.
