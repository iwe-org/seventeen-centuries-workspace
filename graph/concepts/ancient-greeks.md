# Ancient Greeks

## Summary

The ancient Greeks are distinguished by their religious attitude of overflowing gratitude towards nature and life, marking them as a superior type of humanity. This noble stance contrasts with later developments when the populace gained influence, bringing fear into religion and paving the way for Christianity.
