# 💞 Gender Relationships

## Summary

What the books say about love and about the relations between people and between the sexes. Nietzsche's aphorisms on woman and on amour-passion stand beside his suspicion of the love of mankind and of pity; Marcus Aurelius speaks of loving the men among whom one's lot is cast; Machiavelli asks whether it is better for a prince to be loved or feared.
