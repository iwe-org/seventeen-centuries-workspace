# 🧠 Psychology and Instinct

## Summary

The concepts through which the books look inside a human being: fear, shame, suffering, cruelty and the instincts. Nietzsche claims psychology as "the queen of the sciences" and reads morals as a sign-language of the emotions; Marcus Aurelius watches his own ruling faculty and the impressions that reach it; Machiavelli reasons from what men fear and hate.
